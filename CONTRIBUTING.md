# Contributing

Talaria is developed and maintained internally by LIGHTNINGLINK NETWORKS PTE.
LTD. The
[`github.com/X-VPN/Talaria`](https://github.com/X-VPN/Talaria) repository is a
public, read-only mirror of the Talaria source code. We do not accept external
code contributions.

## Pull Requests

We do not review or merge pull requests from external contributors. Pull
requests opened against this repository will be closed without review.

This policy is not a reflection on the quality of any proposed contribution.
Talaria is maintained through our internal development and review process.

## Issues

You are welcome to open issues to report non-security bugs or other technical
issues relating to Talaria.

We may review issues and take them into account in our internal development
process, but we do not commit to responding to, prioritizing, or implementing
any reported issue. Any resulting changes to Talaria are made through our
internal development process and at our discretion.
